Onboarding and time process

Time entry starts only after a completed profile is moved into onboarding. Approved time becomes billable/payable; processed time is the payroll/accounting handoff.

Open Onboarding Open Invoices Open Accounting Current workspace
1. Complete profile Profile, portfolio, personality, and culture must be complete before onboarding.
2. Start onboarding HR creates the onboarding packet from that profile. Candidate receives onboarding and time links.
3. Paperwork submitted Candidate submits start details. The person appears here as an onboarded worker.
4. Time submitted Worker enters weekly hours by day, client/project, and description.
5. Review and approve HR reviews the week. Approved time is ready for invoice/payroll calculations.
6. Process accounting Processing records the payroll batch/reference and feeds Accounting, Invoices, and reports.
Submitted Needs HR review. Do not invoice or pay yet.
Approved Ready to use for customer invoices and resource cost calculations.
Processed Payroll/accounting batch has been recorded. This is the paid/processed ledger state.
